So this is the story of how I got dumped by my ex girlfriend 7 years ago and nearly pissed myself. As always I ask your pardon if my English is strange, but it is not my first language, also I ask you to understand this is a very sad memory for me, maybe it will be cathartic to write about it.

The story is set in full Summer season, it was between the 3rd and the 10th of July. I had been with my then girlfriend Jules (fake name) for nearly 2 years. That winter our relationship was very good (or so I thought at the time), to the point we booked in advance a travel to Crete in late July. Unluckily our relationship started deteriorating with every day that was separating us from the date of our travel.

We were in full exam session at our Uni and I had my last call for Glottology for that session (it was a very difficult one), so I was extra stressed. I also felt something was wrong because Jules had stopped using emoji or sweet words when she texted me and I was unsatisfied by our story in general. I still looked forward to our trip to Crete as a chance to set things or put an end to our relationship. So I was suffering from heat stress and anxiety and I needed to see her to confirm what was happening. Now, my ex lived in an Important city nearby the city I live. By car it is hardly 45 minutes. So whenever we set a date, either I went to her and passed the day there or I went there and picked her to come back to my place, then I took her home and come back again. That time I asked if she wanted to meet to discuss last details of our trip, and she said ‘yes’. She chose to come to my place, eat together, then go out for a walk and to buy some new swimsuits.

I went to her place and picked her up. We went along with our program. She was totally distant, not a single hug, not a single sweet word… we go shopping and I’m starting to feel sick for the summer heat and frustrated by this situation when suddenly, while she was trying some clothes, I have an epifany, one of those moments when you see all your story and realize it’s over. As we exit the shop I confronted her, asked her why she was so distant and finally she admits it: she thinks we are searching different things in life, she probably always considered me as a friend and not a boyfriend and she was postponing this moment until after our trip. She reassured me she understood if I was going to make a scene… I was on the verge of crying right there and then, but all I could think was I refused to give her this pleasure.

There was no way she would see me cry in front of her. So I put on my best poker face, suppresses any human feeling and the turmoil of thoughts in my mind in search of anything to help me go on with my ruse. The first thing I saw was an ice cream shop, so the only ‘logical’ phrase I could say was “Let me buy a granita”. Now I know this is stupid but that was like the only way to save my face in that moment. “Let me buy a granita and then I’ll accompany you home”. The matter of facts is I didn’t want that granita: I had drunk 2L of water for the heat and I had already a very full bladder and knew I had to drive for an hour and a half in total to drive Jules home and then come back to my place. Still I had to do it, I don’t know why, but it was a metter of life or death for me. I forced myself to eat that granita as my bladder expanded and expanded, then I took my ex girlfriend home for the last time in my life.

To get to my car it was a 30 minute ride by tube, then 40 minutes to her house. For all that time my bladder was begging for release. Every road bump, every hole in the street was a direct hit to my exploding bladder. I was sure I couldn’t make it till home, but I was adamant to make it at least to her house not to give her the chance to see me vulnerable. I caressed the thought to ask her to use her bathroom, but it would have been so anticlimactic. So I waited for her to disappear, then tried desperately to head back without leaking.

It was nearly impossible: all the emotions I had suppressed since our talk, all the self loathing were conspiring against my resolve: there was a part of me that thought I deserved further humiliation. I deserved to pee myself: it was the only possible ending for that misadventure. My rational mind was still keeping watch while driving, not to have a car accident and to find a place where I could relieve myself, but I knew that road as I knew my pockets: there was no easy place to stop. Some sort of shield against all that suffering that anesthetized me for many days (I was actually able to cry only after 4 days).
